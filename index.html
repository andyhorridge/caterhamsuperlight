The Car

C7 DFH

A Caterham Superlight built around the idea that less really is more.

Lightweight construction, a six-speed gearbox, limited-slip differential, dry-sump lubrication and uprated suspension and braking make this a Caterham focused firmly on the driving experience.

The car has evolved through its life, including its earlier yellow bodywork before appearing in its distinctive green and exposed carbon-fibre specification.

This site documents the car, its specification, rolling-road sessions, road trips, track days, photographs and video.
