The Car

More than two decades of development.

C7 DFH is a Caterham Superlight purchased on 1 February 2001 and developed progressively over the years into a highly individual, track-focused Seven.

At its heart is a Dave Andrews-built 1.8-litre Rover K-Series VVC engine, featuring extensive internal, induction and lubrication upgrades.

The chassis has received similar attention, with R500 and wide-track suspension components, specially valved Nitron dampers, uprated braking, staggered 13-inch wheels and a limited-slip differential.

Finished in Lamborghini Verde Ithaca , exposed carbon fibre provides the contrast across the nose, front wings and rear wings.

Dave Andrews K-Series

The heart of C7 DFH.

The 1.8-litre VVC engine has been extensively developed rather than simply externally modified.

A flowed cylinder head with larger valves, forged pistons, steel rods and Piper 1144 cams sits at the centre of the specification.

Caterham roller barrels and a carbon airbox handle induction, while the dry-sump system, Titan Gold pump and R500 flywheel complement the engine's track-focused character.

214.8 BHP Emerald rolling road · 17 March 2017

Corrected engine power was recorded at 214.8 bhp, with 173.6 bhp measured at the wheels and 196.7 Nm maximum torque.
