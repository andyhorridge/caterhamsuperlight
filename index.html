C7 DFH
The Car

Built to evolve.

Purchased on 1 February 2001, C7 DFH is a Caterham Superlight developed over more than two decades around the qualities that define a Seven: minimal weight, immediate response and an unfiltered driving experience.

At its heart is a Dave Andrews-built 1.8-litre Rover K-Series VVC engine, paired with Caterham roller-barrel throttle bodies, a six-speed gearbox, limited-slip differential and dry-sump lubrication.

Its Lamborghini Verde Ithaca finish and exposed carbon fibre give the car its present identity. Earlier photographs in the archive preserve its life in yellow, alongside its road trips, track days and development work.
